# Guna Milan matching

**Status:** planned

## Purpose

Score and explain Ashtakoot / Guna Milan compatibility between two charts so families can discuss match strength with classical structure, not only gut feel.

## User need

Users (or families) ask “what is our Guna Milan score,” “how many gunas match,” or “should we proceed,” and need a transparent breakdown of kootas including serious defects such as Nadi.

## Inputs the user provides

- Birth details for both partners (date, time, place) sufficient for Moon/nakshatra-based matching
- A compatibility / Guna Milan / Ashtakoot / kundali-matching question

## What the answer must contain

- Total Guna score out of the classical 36-point Ashtakoot maximum
- Per-koota contribution (Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana, Bhakoot, Nadi) with plain-language meaning
- Explicit call-out when Nadi Dosha (or other traditionally severe defects) is present, including cancellation themes only if the grounding text supports them
- A cautious recommendation frame (“traditionally considered…”) rather than a binding approve/reject decree
- Clear statement when data is insufficient (e.g. unknown birth time affecting derived factors the method requires)

## Grounding source

**Muhurta Chintamani** (and allied Ashtakoot / Guna Milan doctrine) as the source-of-truth for kundali matching once ingested. BPHS career/house corpus alone is not adequate grounding for shipping match scores.

## Out-of-scope

- Synastry systems outside Ashtakoot (composite charts, Western aspect grids) as the primary score
- Legal or religious certification of marriage eligibility
- Reducing the match to a single emoji or “pass/fail” without koota breakdown
- One-chart “will I marry” predictions presented as Guna Milan

## Success signal

Paired-chart golden cases produce the correct total and koota breakdown against a hand-checked Ashtakoot reference, retrieval cites matching-text passages, and matching-tagged quality gates meet the product threshold after Muhurta corpus expansion.
