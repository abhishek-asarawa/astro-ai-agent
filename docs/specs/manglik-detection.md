# Manglik detection

**Status:** planned

## Purpose

Determine whether Mangal Dosha (Manglik / Kuja Dosha) is indicated in the chart and explain marriage-relevant implications and classical mitigations in plain language.

## User need

Users preparing for marriage want a clear yes/no-leaning assessment of Manglik status, how it may affect partnership, and what classical tradition says about cancellation or remedies — without fearmongering.

## Inputs the user provides

- Birth details for the person whose chart is assessed
- Optionally, the partner’s birth details when asking about mutual Manglik impact
- A question about Manglik / Mangal Dosha / Kuja Dosha and marriage

## What the answer must contain

- A plain statement of whether Manglik indicators are present under the adopted classical ruleset
- Which chart factors drive the conclusion (Mars house/sign context), explained without unexplained jargon
- Marriage-relevant implications framed as traditional interpretive themes, not medical or legal claims
- Mention of well-known cancellation or mitigation themes when the grounding text supports them
- Explicit uncertainty when the corpus or rule set is incomplete for the user’s case

## Grounding source

Classical Mangal / Kuja Dosha treatment as found in companion texts planned for corpus expansion (notably **Lal Kitab** remedy framing and standard Parāśari dosha discussion once ingested). Until those sources are available, this feature must not ship as a confident detection product.

## Out-of-scope

- Guaranteeing marriage outcomes or “dosha removal” timelines
- Medical claims about infertility or lifespan
- Regional synonym packs (e.g. Sevvai Dosham) as a first release unless explicitly scoped
- Treating every Mars affliction as Manglik without the classical house criteria

## Success signal

Golden Manglik questions retrieve and cite the correct classical passages, the answer states presence/absence with evidence, and intent/quality gates for dosha-tagged cases meet the product threshold after corpus expansion.
