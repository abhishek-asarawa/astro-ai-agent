# Career guidance

**Status:** live

## Purpose

Help the user understand professional prospects, stability, and near-term work themes from their birth chart, in plain language grounded in classical house and dasha doctrine.

## User need

People ask whether a job change, promotion, business, or career path is supported now, and what to watch for — without needing astrology jargon.

## Inputs the user provides

- Birth details sufficient to cast a chart (date, time, place)
- A career- or work-framed question (job, profession, promotion, business, workplace stability)

## What the answer must contain

- A clear career-oriented summary of the current picture
- Evidence tied to relevant chart factors (especially 10th-house / profession themes and active dasha periods), explained in everyday language
- At least one concrete risk and one concrete opportunity for work life
- Optional practical remedies framed as supportive habits or classical suggestions only when grounded — never invented medical or financial advice
- An explicit confidence cue so the user knows how firm the reading is

## Grounding source

**Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra (BPHS)**, Sharma English Vol.1 — house-effects chapters for profession-related houses and related planetary dignity / lordship doctrine used for career themes.

## Out-of-scope

- Exact employer names, salary figures, or guaranteed hiring outcomes
- Western career-psychology coaching disconnected from the chart
- Education-only questions (admissions, exams) when they are not primarily about profession
- Dosha- or remedy-primary questions that belong to planned dosha / remedy specs

## Success signal

A career question yields a structured career-domain answer that cites chart- and corpus-grounded evidence, stays on profession themes, and remains usable by a non-expert without unexplained technical labels.
