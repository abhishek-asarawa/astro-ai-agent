# Remedies

**Status:** planned

## Purpose

Offer classical, chart-relevant remedial suggestions (gemstone, puja, mantra, yantra, charity) as supportive guidance inside a domain answer — never as guaranteed cures.

## User need

Users ask which stone to wear, which puja or mantra may help, or what Saturday charity is traditional — usually in the context of marriage obstacles, career blocks, or a named dosha.

## Inputs the user provides

- Birth details sufficient to identify relevant planets and afflictions
- A remedy-framed question (gemstone, puja, mantra, yantra, donation/charity) and the life area they hope to support

## What the answer must contain

- Remedies tied to specific chart factors (planet / house / dosha context), not a generic list
- Clear labeling as traditional / cultural suggestions, not medical, financial, or guaranteed outcomes
- Prefer fewer, better-justified suggestions over long catalogs
- When gemstones are discussed: planet correspondence and caution about personal fit; do not prescribe wear schedules as clinical advice
- When the grounding corpus lacks support for the asked remedy type, say so and avoid inventing attributions

## Grounding source

**Lal Kitab** for popular remedy framing; **Ratnashastra** (gemology) for stone–planet correspondence; domain house doctrine from **BPHS** only for linking the life area — remedies must not be hallucinated from BPHS jurisprudence chapters alone.

## Out-of-scope

- Selling or affiliate-promoting specific jewelers or priests
- Medical claims, toxin/“detox” claims, or instructions that could cause harm
- A standalone global `remedies` intent that replaces domain context (remedies stay cross-cutting inside career/health/wealth/family/general)
- Treating charity amounts, mantra counts, or fasting rules as one-size-fits-all mandates

## Success signal

Remedy golden questions retrieve the correct book’s passages (not unrelated BPHS house text), answers name chart-linked suggestions with uncertainty where appropriate, and remedy-tagged retrieval/quality gates pass after Lal Kitab / Ratnashastra ingest.
